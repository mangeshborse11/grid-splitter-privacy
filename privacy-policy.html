Privacy Policy

Grid Splitter for Instagram · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Grid Splitter for Instagram ("the app") is developed by RB Tech Apps. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Your images stay on your device

All image processing happens locally on your phone. The images you choose are never uploaded to our servers, and we do not collect, store or share them.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal details. The app does not use analytics of its own.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and general usage data to show and measure ads, and to personalise them where permitted. You can manage ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchase

The app offers a one-time purchase to remove ads. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Permissions

The app uses internet access to display ads and process the purchase. Images are picked through the system file picker and saved or shared through the Android share sheet.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
